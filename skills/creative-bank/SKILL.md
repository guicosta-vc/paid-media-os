# Creative Bank

Use this skill to create a source-backed bank of 50 paid creative concepts, then choose an initial 3-5-ad test cell. It produces copy and production requirements, not published ads or generated media.

## Preconditions

Read an approved Strategy Lock. Confirm one conversion action, buyer boundary, landing promise, claim ledger, and approval owner. Treat missing proof as a blocker or an explicit assumption.

Before accepting any uploaded input, require the operator to remove credentials, personal data, confidential customer material, private call transcripts, unapproved screenshots, and non-public campaign exports. Confirm that they have permission to use every identifiable person, logo, testimonial, customer quote, and evidence item.

## Procedure

1. Build ten categories with five concepts each: positioning, pain, desired outcome, three mechanisms, use cases, one capability, proof or objection, and retargeting.
   Completion: exactly 50 stable IDs exist.
2. For every concept, record funnel, awareness, ICP signal, trigger, claim status, on-image headline, primary text, CTA, hypothesis, visual job, and media kind.
   Completion: no concept lacks a required field or exceeds the claim ledger.
3. Verify uniqueness, headline clarity, CTA alignment, category coverage, and proof safety.
   Completion: every concept is eligible, revise, or rejected.
4. Select 3-5 eligible concepts with materially distinct hypotheses and feasible assets.
   Completion: the selection explains why near-misses were not selected.
5. Create a production handoff. For static, carousel, or video, specify format, placement, approved assets, rights limits, visual job, and exact copy.
   Completion: every selected item can be produced without inventing proof or product UI.

## Rules

- A 50-concept bank is not a 50-ad launch instruction.
- A video is an approved asset type, not a promise that this system will generate or edit video.
- Never create a visual asset, upload media, or call Meta without a separate approval.
